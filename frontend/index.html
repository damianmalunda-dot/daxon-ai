ABOUT DAXON
🚀 DAXON AI — YOUR STUDY ASSISTANT
DAXON AI is a modern digital platform built by Daxon Technology, designed to help every student and small business owner in Tanzania study more easily, understand faster, and work more efficiently — with no extra costs and no API keys required.
Every tool here — from reading documents and scanning images to translating languages and summarizing information from the web — runs directly in your browser, quickly and with complete privacy, since all your data stays on your device.
🎯 OUR MISSION
To empower Tanzanian students and entrepreneurs with cutting-edge technology in their own language — so education and progress are never held back by cost or language barriers.
🔒 PRIVACY
When you turn on Privacy Mode, DAXON AI will stop sending any requests to the internet. Everything will run entirely on your device — using only your Notes, granted phone files, and uploaded files.
